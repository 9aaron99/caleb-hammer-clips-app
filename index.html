Caleb Hammer Clips

Clips production tool

Internal, single-operator content pipeline

Caleb Hammer Clips is a personal content-production tool: it transcribes long-form financial-audit videos, identifies strong short-form clip candidates, renders vertical clips with captions, and publishes approved clips to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts accounts.

This is not a public product or service — it is internal tooling operated by a single creator for their own content workflow, with every publish requiring manual review and approval before it goes live.
