Caleb Hammer Clips is a personal, single-operator content tool used to create and publish short-form video clips derived from publicly available financial-audit podcast and stream content, for posting to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

1. Use of the app

This application is operated by an individual creator for their own content production workflow. It is not offered as a public service, and access is not provided to third parties.

2. Content

The app processes video the operator has the rights or permission to use, generates derivative short-form clips, and publishes them to the operator's own connected social media accounts via each platform's official API. No content is published without the operator's manual review and approval.

3. Platform integrations

This app connects to TikTok's Content Posting API, and in the future to other platform APIs, solely to publish content to accounts the operator owns and controls. The app does not access, modify, or publish to any account other than the operator's own.
