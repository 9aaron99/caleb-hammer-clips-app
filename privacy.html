Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

This app is a personal, single-operator content production tool. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from third parties or the general public.

1. What data this app handles

The app processes source video files, generated transcripts, and video clips supplied by the operator. When connected to a platform such as TikTok via that platform's official API, the app stores only what is required to authenticate as the operator and publish content on the operator's own behalf: OAuth access and refresh tokens, the operator's platform account identifier, and a local record of upload attempts and their results.

2. Storage

All credentials and tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device, outside of any shared or synced project directory, and are never transmitted to any party other than the platform's own official API endpoints.

3. Sharing

No data collected or generated by this app is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. The app communicates only with the official APIs of the platforms the operator explicitly connects, such as TikTok's Content Posting API.

4. User control

The operator can revoke the app's access to any connected platform account at any time through that platform's own account settings, and can delete locally stored tokens and records directly.
